# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Product overview

BookMyQ is a digital queue and appointment platform for businesses that need real-time service coordination. It integrates customer self-service, queue operations, branch-level resource management, and AI-based optimization.

## Target personas

- Super Admin: manages multi-tenant platform controls
- Platform Admin: configures policies and deployment-level operations
- Tenant Owner: owns branding and operational rules
- Branch Manager: oversees branch staffing and service execution
- Service Manager: manages queue and service flows
- Receptionist: handles walk-ins and front-office operations
- Agent/Executive: supports assigned customers and services
- Customer: books and tracks appointment status
- Support Team: resolves operational issues and customer concerns

## Functional priorities

1. Customer registration and appointment booking
2. Token generation and queue management
3. Real-time dashboard and operations visibility
4. AI-powered queue optimization
5. Multi-tenant onboarding and branch configuration
6. Billing and subscription management
7. Notifications and digital check-in
8. CRM and analytics integrations

## User journeys

### Customer journey

1. Customer downloads app or visits portal
2. Selects service, branch, and preferred time
3. Books appointment or uses walk-in check-in
4. Receives confirmation and queue status notifications
5. Checks in digitally and receives updates in real time

### Staff journey

1. Staff logs in and loads queue dashboard
2. Relevant customer queue is assigned to an available agent
3. Service state and queue status are updated in real time
4. Delays and SLA breaches are highlighted to supervisors

## Success metrics

- Improved customer satisfaction score
- Faster service completion rate
- Reduced no-show rate through reminder automation
- Better resource utilization across branches
