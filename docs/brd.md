# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## Executive summary

BookMyQ is a SaaS platform that modernizes customer flow for service-driven organizations. The solution optimizes queue handling, reduces waiting time, and creates a structured digital appointment experience for both staff and customers.

## Business goals

- Reduce wait times across service counters and branches
- Increase customer satisfaction and retention
- Automate queue and scheduling processes
- Support enterprise multi-tenant operations across locations
- Enable digital engagement for customers and staff

## Business drivers

- Growing expectation for digital self-service experiences
- Need for operational consistency across branch networks
- Demand for reduced manual queue management overhead
- Need for real-time workforce and capacity planning

## Scope

The platform supports queue generation, customer registration, resource allocation, branch operations, digital check-in, CRM integration, notifications, analytics, and AI recommendation capabilities.

## Business stakeholders

- Platform leadership
- Regional service managers
- Tenant owners
- Branch operators
- Customer success teams
- Support and operations teams

## KPI targets

- Reduce average customer wait time by 30-50%
- Increase appointment conversion by 20%
- Improve SLA compliance to 95%+
- Increase staff productivity through queue automation
- Deliver 99.9% service availability for core workflows

## Assumptions

- Tenants operate in multiple locations with diverse service lines
- Customers and staff require digital and physical coordinated workflows
- Data privacy and operational auditability are essential for enterprise adoption
